The listening gap

Knowing the term is not the same as hearing it.

Students can learn definitions of instruments, intervals, textures and metres, then still struggle to recognise those features in unfamiliar extracts.

Most revision resources teach students what musical terms mean. EchoAural trains them to recognise those features when they actually hear them.
